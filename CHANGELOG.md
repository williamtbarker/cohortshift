# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-02

### Added

- Expanding-window temporal folds with configurable frequency, gaps, and test windows.
- Optional train-side purging of entities repeated in future test cohorts.
- Schema, time, target-copy, missingness, constant, cardinality, and duplicate audits.
- Fold-local numeric and categorical preprocessing with class-balanced logistic regression.
- Classification, probability, calibration, and aggregate fold metrics.
- Numeric PSI and categorical Jensen–Shannon feature-drift reports.
- Source/frame fingerprints, environment provenance, predictions, and coefficient summaries.
- Atomic owner-only report artifacts.
- Deterministic synthetic dataset generator, 31 tests, CI, and wheel verification.
